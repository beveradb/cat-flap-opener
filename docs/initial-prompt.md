# Initial prompt

The original request that started this project (2026-09-28), recorded verbatim.

---

our cat, OMalley, struggles to open the cat flap, and i think it puts him off going outside when he should. he's an orange cat... sometimes he hops up onto his shelf to go outside, but doesn't like the feeling of pushing the flap with his head so ends up not going out, or i cave and walk over to hold the flap open for him till he sniffs the air and decides to walk through to the outside...
anyway, i'd like to build something which opens it for him automatically when he comes near it. I reckon we could build / i'm imagining something using a raspberry pi (or nano/embedded version of one) or something hooked up to a camera and a tiny on-device classifier model or something to detect cat and trigger a GPIO pin output to power a tiny low powered motor or something with some string/fishing line connected to a motor sitting on top of the cat flap (it's mounted in my living room window in a DIY acrylic frame right now so the motor might instead be mounted on the wooden window a couple inches higher) and on the other end of the string/line would be the bottom of the inner cat flap (the movable flap), so the motor running for a estimated-then-tuned number of seconds would open the cat flap fully, over about 2 seconds. 
an alternate design for the electronics if doable could be a simple esp32 hooked up to the motor, then we send events to the esp32 from homeassistant based on frigate events, with frigate ingesting video from one new cheap indoor tapo camera (i already have several in other places around the house, already have frigate set up, etc. - see /Users/andrew/Projects/beveradb/home-servers-config for context). however sometimes i stop being on top of fixing issues in my frigate setup and haven't maintained my homeassistant setup properly for over a year; it works (i still use it daily to turn lights on/off) but my confidence in its reliability isn't great so i like the idea of a fully standalone device a bit more, if we can do it.

this will of course need to be designed to be something which won't be of any risk to the cat, and a minimalistic low voltage/low power/battery powered design etc. ideally so worst case scenario if he gets interested in the mechanism and swipes at it there's no real risk to him. if that's unrealistic or doesn't result in reasonable pricing for the battery or super low runtime days between recharges, i'm open to mains with a low voltage DC adapter or USB power supply or something to power this but ideally we should have the bare minimum of tech mounted/visible on the actual window/cat flap, to avoid attracting his attention/risk of swipes but also to avoid it looking uglyer than the cat window already does in our living room! we can run a couple of wires from the mount point on the window, along and down the wall to the single board computer or whatever we settle on - anything more techy running/plugged into the wall outlet out of sight. ideally the tech mounted on the window would be no more than a tiny motor and a tiny camera, perhaps glued directly together and mounted with a couple of screws directly to the window. if this ends up being realistic maybe you could help me find and buy all the components for this build?

i've created new dir cat-flap-opener for this, let's make it into a new public repo in my github and start committing/pushing docs (including this verbatim initial prompt) once we lock in the feasibility and rough design.
